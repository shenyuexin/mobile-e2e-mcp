## Re-Test Rule

Any future wording/frontmatter change to the real skill candidate requires re-running the publication-grade acceptance card for that lane.

## Fail Conditions

Do not publish if:

- RED no longer fails meaningfully without guidance,
- GREEN no longer shows a materially better diagnosis,
- invocation boundaries blur across baseline/platform skills.
